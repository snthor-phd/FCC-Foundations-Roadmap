AN INVITATION FROM PASTOR MATT

Come Help Us Discover What
Faircreek Adults Need

We're beginning to imagine how Faircreek can strengthen adult spiritual formation — not merely by adding another class, but by building a reproducible pathway that helps adults learn and practice the rhythms of life with Christ. Before we decide anything, we want to gather spiritually mature leaders and potential teachers to discern the real needs of our church and to notice where God may already be equipping people to serve. Would you come and explore it with us?

WHO

Elders & wives, former elders, deacon-like leaders, and potential teachers

WHEN
WHERE

Before You Come

No homework — just a few quiet minutes of prayerful thought. We'll begin the meeting by writing down our reflections, so come having pondered:

  • Where do you sense a Holy-Spirit-led need for adult spiritual growth at Faircreek — and what would you suggest?
  • Where do you believe you could make an impact?
  • What are you burdened to teach, model, or facilitate?
  • What resources come to mind that you'd want to use if you taught a piece of this?

What Participation Means

This is exploratory. Saying no is an honorable outcome. Saying yes does not mean immediate teaching.

The pilot may involve short (three- to six-week) modules, co-leaders, and reviewed resources. Leaders teach from their own passion and strength — the church offers possible resources and a simple template; you make the choices.

Notes are gathered along the way so Faircreek can build a repeatable pathway for years to come.

The Practices We'll Explore

YEAR ONE · ROOTED
Apprenticeship / The Way·Scripture·Prayer·Community
YEAR TWO · SENT
Serving·Personal Testimony / Witness (incl. baptism)·Generosity

Classic practices such as Sabbath, solitude, and fasting are under review — and leaders' passions may add more.

If God is stirring something in you, He may well be preparing you to do something about it. Come, pray, and listen with us.

Grateful for you,

Pastor Matt
